01 What it is

Bourse is a small stock exchange and a trading robot, built to be checked rather than trusted. The exchange matches buyers and sellers strictly by price and then by arrival time, never loses an order, and gives the same answer every time it sees the same orders. The robot tries to earn the gap between buying and selling prices, and is judged by tests that assume every profit is luck or cheating until shown otherwise.
